    Experience¶

    Stewarded by the Firefly Software Foundation. dev@fireflysoftware.io

    A journey is what a person or an employee does. It is not a copy of a domain.

    Accounts, cards, and lending stay separate. Payments are their own journey. They are not a step inside the account journey. Insurance is its own journey: quote, bind, service, and claim. The contracts that already have an OpenAPI file are under API.

    Banking¶

    • Open and close an account
    • Cards
    • Lending
    • Finance terminal. An employee signs in, the terminal checks administer, and impersonation stores the employee id and the customer id.

    Shared and insurance¶

    • Onboard a person or a company
    • Customer profile
    • Insurance. Quote, bind, and claim. The policy and underwriting domains do not have an endorse saga or a renew saga.
    • Insurance terminal. An employee signs in, the terminal checks administer, and impersonation stores the employee id and the customer id.
