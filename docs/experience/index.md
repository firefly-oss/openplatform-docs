A journey is what a person or an employee does. It is not a copy of a domain.

Accounts, cards, and lending stay separate. Payments are their own journey. They are not a step inside the account journey. Insurance is its own journey: quote, bind, service, and claim. The contracts that already have an OpenAPI file are under [API](../api/index.md).
