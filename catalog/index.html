    Families¶

    Stewarded by the Firefly Software Foundation. dev@fireflysoftware.io

    The OpenPlatform release list contains 90 repositories. This catalog documents all 90. Each entry names the repository, the layer, and what that repository owns. Links go to github.com/fireflysoftwaresolutions.

    Insurance is one of the families, with seven domain services and seven cores. A lending core with no domain-lending-* repository is described as a core without a domain orchestrator.

    Service host pattern: https://openplatform.fireflysoftware.io/{service}. Public site: fireflysoftware.io. Publisher: Firefly Software Foundation. Contact: dev@fireflysoftware.io.

    Customer and party¶

    • core-common-customer-mgmt Core. Owns party, natural person, and legal entity rows, plus industry classification, party attributes, and relationship edges.

    • core-common-kycb-mgmt Core. Owns KYC, KYB, and AML screening records.

    • domain-customer-kyc-kyb Domain. Owns the KYC and KYB saga: open a case, attach evidence, and change status, with compensation on create, through core-common-kycb-mgmt.

    • domain-customer-people Domain. Owns the party saga: create, update, status change, and relationship changes for a natural person or a legal entity, with compensation, through core-common-customer-mgmt.

    Contract¶

    • core-common-contract-mgmt Core. Owns contract rows, contract lines, status history, and predecessor links.

    • domain-common-contracts Domain. Owns the contract saga: create, update, status change, and delete.

    Product¶

    • core-common-product-mgmt Core. Owns products and optional product configuration, including pricing rows.

    • domain-core-pricing-engine Domain. Calculates a price, a commission, and eligibility from product configuration.

    • domain-product-catalog Domain. Owns the product saga: register, publish, suspend, resume, and retire, through core-common-product-mgmt.

    • domain-product-pricing Domain. Owns optional pricing configuration on a product.

    Banking¶

    • core-banking-accounts Core. Owns account records, balances, parameters, providers, spaces, and restrictions.

    • core-banking-cards Core. Owns payment-card records, including physical and virtual cards.

    • core-banking-ledger Core. Owns ledger transactions.

    • core-banking-payments Core. Owns payment orders, instructions, schedules, and related payment records.

    • core-banking-psdx Core. Owns open-banking access records for account information and payment initiation, including consent.

    • domain-banking-accounts Domain. Owns the account saga: open, update, and close, with compensation, through core-banking-accounts.

    • domain-banking-cards Domain. Owns the card saga: issue, activate, block, replace, and cancel, with compensation, through core-banking-cards.

    Lending¶

    • core-lending-asset-finance Core. Owns asset-finance agreements, including leasing and renting terms, and the financed asset.

    • core-lending-collateral-management Core. Owns collateral assets, cases, liens, valuations, and monitoring.

    • core-lending-credit-cards Core. Owns revolving credit lines, billing cycles, statements, and amounts posted to the credit line.

    • core-lending-credit-scoring Core. Owns scoring cases and bureau-call records.

    • core-lending-delinquency-collections Core. Owns delinquency cases, collection actions, and payment promises.

    • core-lending-loan-origination Core. Owns loan applications, parties on the application, documents, proposed offers, underwriting decisions, scores, and status history.

    • core-lending-loan-servicing Core. Owns servicing after origination: disbursements, repayment schedules, interest, rate changes, and servicing events.

    • core-lending-mortgage Core. Owns mortgage agreement terms.

    • core-lending-personal-loans Core. Owns personal-loan contractual terms, including purpose and early-repayment conditions.

    • core-lending-provisioning-risk Core. Owns provisioning cases, expected-credit-loss amounts, and risk assessments.

    • core-lending-regulatory-compliance Core. Owns AML cases, suspicious-activity reports, and regulatory report records.

    • core-lending-supply-chain-finance Core. Owns supply-chain finance agreements, including factoring and confirming.

    • domain-lending-asset-finance Domain. Owns the asset-finance saga through core-lending-asset-finance.

    • domain-lending-collateral-management Domain. Owns the collateral saga: create, update, status change, and close, with compensation, through core-lending-collateral-management.

    • domain-lending-credit-scoring Domain. Owns the scoring saga through core-lending-credit-scoring.

    • domain-lending-delinquency-collections Domain. Owns the collections saga: create, update, status change, and close, with compensation, through core-lending-delinquency-collections.

    • domain-lending-loan-origination Domain. Owns the origination saga: submit, update, and record an underwriting outcome, through core-lending-loan-origination.

    • domain-lending-loan-servicing Domain. Owns the servicing saga: disburse, repay, and later servicing commands, through core-lending-loan-servicing.

    • domain-lending-mortgage Domain. Owns the mortgage agreement saga: create, update, status change, and close, with compensation, through core-lending-mortgage.

    • domain-lending-personal-loans Domain. Owns the personal-loan agreement saga through core-lending-personal-loans.

    • domain-lending-provisioning-risk Domain. Owns the provisioning saga: create, update, status change, and close, with compensation, through core-lending-provisioning-risk.

    • domain-lending-regulatory-compliance Domain. Owns the AML-case saga: create, update, status change, and close, with compensation, through core-lending-regulatory-compliance.

    • domain-lending-supply-chain-finance Domain. Owns the supply-chain finance saga: create, update, status change, and close, with compensation, through core-lending-supply-chain-finance.

    Insurance¶

    • core-insurance-claims Core. Owns claim files, coverage checks, reserves, assessments, settlements, fraud referrals, and recoveries.

    • core-insurance-delegated-authority Core. Owns binders, authority limits, and coverholder appointments.

    • core-insurance-financials Core. Owns premium schedules, commissions, refunds, bordereaux, premium audits, and credit-control records.

    • core-insurance-policies Core. Owns policy records, parties on the policy, transactions, coverages, certificates, and line-level risk details.

    • core-insurance-reinsurance Core. Owns reinsurance treaties, cessions, and claim recoveries recorded against those treaties.

    • core-insurance-reserving Core. Owns reserving snapshots, loss-development triangle cells, and reporting extracts.

    • core-insurance-underwriting Core. Owns underwriting submissions, clearance, statements of value, loss runs, quotes, referrals, and underwriting decisions.

    • domain-insurance-claims Domain. Owns the claims saga: create, update, status change, and close, with compensation, through core-insurance-claims.

    • domain-insurance-delegated-authority Domain. Owns the delegated-authority saga: create, update, status change, and close a binder, with compensation, through core-insurance-delegated-authority.

    • domain-insurance-financials Domain. Owns the financials saga for premium schedules and related billing records, through core-insurance-financials.

    • domain-insurance-policies Domain. Owns the policy saga: create, update, status change, and close, with compensation, through core-insurance-policies.

    • domain-insurance-reinsurance Domain. Owns the reinsurance saga for treaties and cessions, through core-insurance-reinsurance.

    • domain-insurance-reserving Domain. Owns the reserving saga for snapshots and reporting extracts, through core-insurance-reserving.

    • domain-insurance-underwriting Domain. Owns the underwriting saga for submissions, quotes, referrals, and decisions, through core-insurance-underwriting.

    Data and external systems¶

    • domain-data-business-intelligence Domain. Owns the port for company lookup against an external business-information provider.

    • domain-data-credit-bureaus Domain. Owns the port for a credit-bureau report.

    Security and access¶

    • app-authenticator Application. Gateway in front of the security center.

    • core-common-sca-mgmt Core. Owns strong-customer-authentication operations, challenges, attempts, and the audit trail.

    • core-common-security-vault Core. Owns credential and secret records, including rotation and an access audit.

    • core-common-user-mgmt Core. Owns users, roles, and permissions.

    • domain-common-sca Domain. Owns the strong-customer-authentication saga: start a challenge, verify the response, and write the audit event, through core-common-sca-mgmt.

    • domain-core-security-center Domain. Owns authentication sessions.

    • exp-security Experience. Owns the sign-in journey: password, biometric sign-in, session, strong customer authentication, and password change.

    Onboarding and other journeys¶

    • bfc-initconfig Application. Channel startup service.

    • exp-accounts Experience. Owns the account journey: open, summary, list, and close.

    • exp-cards Experience. Owns the card journey: issue, summary, list, activate, block, and cancel.

    • exp-channel-management Experience. Owns channel startup reads: branding, languages, and reference data in one response.

    • exp-distributor Experience. Owns the distributor-portal journey: registration, branding, catalog, territories, and agents.

    • exp-lending Experience. Owns lending screens from simulation and application through offer, signing, and servicing.

    • exp-notifications Experience. Owns the in-app notification journey: list, read, dismiss, unread count, and preferences.

    • exp-onboarding Experience. Owns onboarding for a natural person and for a legal entity.

    • exp-profile Experience. Owns the signed-in customer profile journey: read and update the person, contacts, documents, consents, and contract summaries.

    Shared platform¶

    • app-configurator-masters Application. Gateway in front of configuration.

    • core-common-accounting-gl Core. Owns general-ledger accounting records for the platform.

    • core-common-config-mgmt Core. Owns tenant configuration, provider settings, channel settings, and feature flags.

    • core-common-distributor-mgmt Core. Owns distributor records, agreements, agencies, agents, territories, and catalog rows.

    • core-common-document-mgmt Core. Owns document metadata and stored content.

    • core-common-modelhub Core. Owns data models defined at runtime, and the records stored against those models.

    • core-common-notification-services Core. Owns notification messages and delivery state.

    • core-common-org-mgmt Core. Owns organization structure: divisions, regions, branches, departments, positions, and working calendars.

    • core-common-reference-master-data Core. Owns reference lists such as countries, currencies, and document types.

    • domain-common-notifications Domain. Owns the notification saga: send, record history, and store channel preferences, with compensation, through core-common-notification-services.

    • domain-core-configuration Domain. Owns configuration queries: languages, lookup domains, tenant branding, and master-data lookup items.

    • domain-distributor-branding Domain. Owns the distributor saga for legal identity, branding, terms, agencies, agents, and territories, through core-common-distributor-mgmt.

    • domain-distributor-catalog Domain. Owns the saga for a distributor product catalog, through core-common-distributor-mgmt.

    • firefly-bom Platform. Bill of materials for version 26.10.4.

    • firefly-parent Platform. Parent POM for OpenPlatform modules.

    • library-common-batch Library. Library for scheduling batch jobs, with optional execution audit and a lock so the same job does not run concurrently on two nodes.

    • library-psps Library. Port library for payment service providers.

    • library-rails Library. Port library for payment rails.

    Decision¶

    No separate decision repository. The published decision service is domain-lending-credit-scoring. See Decision.
